2. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
A. Hardware Requirements
1. Processor: Intel Core i3 or above.
2. RAM: Minimum 4 GB.
3. Hard Disk: Minimum 10 GB free space.
4. System: Desktop or Laptop.
B. Software Requirements
1. Operating System: Windows 10/11.
2. Database: MySQL.
3. Database Management Tool: MySQL Workbench.
4. Programming Language: SQL.
5. Documentation Tool: MS Word.
C. Functional Requirements
1. Customer Management: Store and manage customer details.
2. Restaurant Management: Maintain restaurant information.
3. Food Management: Store food names, categories and prices.
4. Order Management: Maintain customer order details.
5. Payment Management: Store payment methods and payment status.
6. Delivery Management: Maintain delivery partner information.
7. Review Management: Store customer ratings and feedback.
8. Sales Analysis: Calculate total revenue and monthly sales.
9. Customer Analysis: Identify frequent customers.
10. Restaurant Analysis: Identify top-performing restaurants.
D. Non-Functional Requirements
1. Performance: Execute SQL queries efficiently.
2. Security: Protect database information.
3. Reliability: Maintain accurate records.
4. Scalability: Support additional data.
5. Data Integrity: Maintain consistency using keys and constraints.
6. Maintainability: Allow easy database updates.
